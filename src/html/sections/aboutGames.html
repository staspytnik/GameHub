About the Game

An open-world action-adventure story set in Night City, a megalopolis obsessed with power, glamour and body modification. You play as V, a mercenary outlaw going after a one-of-a-kind implant that is the key to immortality.

With breathtaking visuals and an immersive soundtrack, Cyberpunk 2077 represents the pinnacle of modern game design. Whether you are a veteran or a newcomer to the genre, this title offers hundreds of hours of content and replayability that few games can match.
